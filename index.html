Privacy & Security

Your meetings stay private. By design.

Blue Mic was built privacy-first. Meeting audio is encrypted, access is restricted to authenticated users, and any sharing happens through secure, expiring codes, never public links that can leak, get indexed, or be opened by anyone who stumbles on them.

US data residency

All data is stored and processed in the United States, on AWS.

Never used to train AI

Your transcripts and meetings run through the AI providers' business APIs, never their consumer products, and none of them train their models on your data.

Authenticated access only

Meetings are only accessible to authenticated Blue Mic users on your team, never exposed on the open web.

No public exposure

No link previews, no browser history, no accidental indexing. Your meeting content stays private.

Tenant-isolated

Each organization's meetings, notes, and settings are isolated to that tenant, so teams never see each other's data.

Encrypted in transit & at rest

Audio, transcripts, and notes are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), with public access blocked.

Why this matters. Many transcription tools share meetings through public web links, and the moment one of those URLs ends up somewhere it shouldn't, the meeting is gone:

  • Public links / "anyone with a link" means a leaked URL = leaked meeting content
  • Web viewers get crawled, cached, and screenshotted by link-preview bots
  • Browser history and shared devices can persist content long after the share is "revoked"
  • No authentication requirement means no accountability for who accessed what

Blue Mic was designed to close every one of those gaps.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do reps capture meetings? +
Two ways. On the road, reps record with the Blue Mic app for iOS and Android: one tap, and Blue Mic produces the transcript and summary automatically. In the office on another recorder or notetaker, they simply paste the transcript into Blue Mic and run the exact same coaching, notes, and follow-up workflow.
What platforms is Blue Mic available on? +
Blue Mic is available as an iPhone app, an Android app, and a web app. Reps record and run the full workflow on iOS and Android in the field, and the web app gives your team browser access to meetings, Sales Coach, and (coming soon) manager dashboards.
Which CRMs does Blue Mic connect to? +
Blue Mic syncs summary notes, follow-up tasks, and drafted emails to Bullhorn and HubSpot today, with Salesforce and Avionté on the roadmap. Don't see yours? We'd like to hear about it. Email info@blue-mic.com.
What does Sales Coach actually do? +
After every meeting, Sales Coach surfaces the top wins, scores your priority objectives (Problem, Impact & Point of View) to earn the Blue Mic Trifecta Award, and gives coaching suggestions on what was missed or could improve. It also drafts the follow-up emails and the CRM-ready summary note, so the rep just reviews and sends.
Do we have to change how our team already records meetings? +
No. If your reps already use another meeting recorder or transcription tool, they can paste that transcript straight into Blue Mic and still get the full coaching, notes, and follow-up workflow.
Is Blue Mic available to individuals? +
Blue Mic is focused on Enterprise sales teams. Access is provisioned for your organization rather than sold individually. Contact info@blue-mic.com to get your team set up.
How is our data protected? +
Meeting audio is encrypted in transit and at rest in AWS with public access blocked, each organization's data is isolated to its own tenant, and meetings are only accessible to authenticated Blue Mic users, never exposed on the open web. See the security section above for the full picture.
